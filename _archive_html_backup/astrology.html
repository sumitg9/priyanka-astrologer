Cosmic Blueprint & Personal Clarity

Vedic Astrology, Thoughtfully Interpreted.

Vedic Astrology (Jyotish) is the ancient Indian science of light and time. Rooted in the timeless Parashari and Jaimini traditions, a genuine astrological consultation is far more than generic sun-sign predictions—it is a meticulous, mathematical study of celestial geometries calculated at your exact moment of birth.

By charting your Rising Sign (Lagna), Moon Sign (Rashi), the 27 Nakshatras (lunar mansions), and the 12 Bhavas (houses), Priyanka Dey reveals the underlying planetary currents shaping your temperament, vocational strengths, relationship dynamics, and life cycles. Rather than fostering fear or passive fatalism, our sessions empower you to navigate life's seasons with self-assurance and strategic timing.

  • Precise Birth Chart Geometry: Sidereal planetary calculations mapping your Lagna, Dashamsha (D10 career), and Navamsha (D9 relationships).
  • Cosmic Timeline & Dashas: Detailed breakdown of your Vimshottari Mahadasha and Antardasha cycles to pinpoint favorable phases.
  • Current Transits (Gochara): Assessing the active influences of Saturn (Shani Sade Sati), Jupiter transits, and Rahu-Ketu karmic axes.
  • Actionable Vedic Remedies: Sattvic, practical remedies including tailored mantras, mindful routines, charity (Daana), and gemstone feasibility.
Astrology is an interpretive philosophical science designed to cultivate self-awareness and conscious action. Consultations are honest, compassionate, and strictly free from fear-based superstitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Astrology Sessions.

Clear, upfront answers to common questions about preparing for your Vedic reading.

What exact details are required for an astrology consultation? +

To generate your authentic Vedic birth chart (Janam Kundali), three exact coordinates are required:

  • Date of Birth: Day, month, and year.
  • Exact Time of Birth: Recorded as accurately as possible (hours and minutes), ideally from a hospital birth record or official birth certificate. Because the rising sign (Lagna) changes roughly every two hours, accurate birth time is vital.
  • Place of Birth: The city, town, or district where you were born to calculate latitude and longitude.
What if I do not know my exact birth time? +

If your birth time is only known within an approximate window (for example, between 2:00 PM and 3:00 PM), Priyanka can perform Birth Time Rectification (BTR). This involves cross-verifying key milestone dates in your life—such as major educational achievements, marriage date, major career events, or surgical procedures—against astrological divisional charts to pinpoint your exact Lagna degree.

Alternatively, for pressing immediate questions where birth time is completely unknown, Prashna Kundali (Horary Astrology) can be cast for the exact moment your query is presented.

How does Vedic Astrology differ from Western Astrology? +

The fundamental difference lies in the celestial coordinate system. Vedic Astrology (Jyotish) utilizes the Sidereal Zodiac, which accounts for the precession of the equinoxes and maps planetary positions against the actual, visible constellations in the sky. Western astrology typically relies on the Tropical Zodiac, which is fixed to the changing seasons of the Earth.

Furthermore, Vedic Astrology places profound importance on your Moon Sign (Rashi) and Ascendant (Lagna) rather than just the Sun sign, and employs the 27 Nakshatras and the predictive Vimshottari Dasha system, providing nuanced timing for life developments.

Are your astrological remedies expensive, fear-based, or superstitious? +

No, absolutely not. Priyanka Dey strictly adheres to ethical Vedic principles and firmly rejects fear-mongering predictions or predatory commercial rituals.

Remedies suggested are classical, sattvic, and empowering: including personalized Vedic mantras for mental equanimity, mindfulness routines, charitable acts (Daana) aligned with planetary energies, dietary adjustments, and authentic natural gemstone recommendations only when genuinely beneficial and feasible for the client.

The astrological calculation, chart depth, and personalized attention in an online video session are 100% identical to an in-person visit to our Siliguri chamber. You are welcome to take notes or request a brief written summary of key points.

Can I ask specific questions about sensitive personal, career, or family matters? +

Yes. During your 60-minute session, you have full freedom to ask specific questions about any life dimension—including career shifts, partnership hurdles, business partnerships, financial worries, childbirth timing, or health cycles.

We recommend writing down your key questions before the consultation so that every priority is thoroughly addressed.

Your privacy is paramount. All birth data, discussion points, personal circumstances, and consultation notes are strictly confidential and are never disclosed, shared, or utilized for any secondary purpose.
