Consultation Agreement & Terms

By scheduling or participating in an astrology, Kundali, tarot, numerology, or Vastu consultation with Priyanka Dey, you agree to the following terms and principles.

1. Nature of Consultations

Astrology, tarot reading, Vastu Shastra, and numerology are ancient interpretive and spiritual traditions. They are provided as philosophical, reflective guidance to assist with self-awareness, personal perspective, and timing clarity. They do not claim to foretell an immutable future or guarantee deterministic outcomes.

2. Client Eligibility & Responsibility

  • Clients must be at least 18 years of age to book a consultation.
  • You are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of birth dates, times, and locations provided. Vedic calculations depend heavily on precise birth data.
  • All decisions, actions, and lifestyle choices you undertake following a reading remain your sole personal responsibility.

3. Professional Disclaimer

Consultations with Priyanka Dey are not a substitute for qualified professional medical, psychiatric, legal, or licensed financial advisory services. For acute health concerns, legal disputes, or substantial capital investments, you should always consult accredited professionals in their respective licensed disciplines.

4. Appointments & Punctuality

Consultations are conducted in-person in Siliguri, West Bengal, or online via video call (Google Meet / Zoom / WhatsApp). Please arrive or connect on time. If you need to reschedule, please provide at least 24 hours notice as outlined in our Refund & Rescheduling Policy.

5. Respectful Code of Conduct

We are dedicated to providing an empathetic, safe, and respectful environment. We reserve the right to decline or terminate a consultation in the rare event of abusive, hostile, or inappropriate behavior, without refund.
