Refund & Rescheduling Policy

Transparent guidelines regarding appointment scheduling, preparation time, and cancellations.

Last updated: November 2026

Appointment Scheduling & Fee Policy

Each Vedic astrology reading, Kundali breakdown, and Vastu audit involves dedicated time, mathematical transit calculations, and spiritual preparation before the live session occurs. Our policy balances respect for the consultant's preparation time with understanding for unexpected client circumstances.

1. Rescheduling an Appointment

Life can be unpredictable. You may reschedule your consultation up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled appointment time at no additional charge. We will gladly find another convenient mutual date and time slot.

2. Cancellation & Refund Eligibility

  • Notice Given > 24 Hours in Advance: If you cancel your booking with at least 24 hours notice before the appointment, you are eligible for a full refund minus standard payment gateway transaction charges (if applicable).
  • Notice Given < 24 Hours: Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice are eligible for rescheduling rather than a cash refund, as prior chart preparation will have already been completed.
  • Missed Appointments (No-Show): If a client does not attend their scheduled appointment without prior notification, the session fee is non-refundable.

3. Completed Consultations

Consultation fees compensate the consultant for their expertise, chart casting calculations, and time dedicated during the session. Once a consultation has been conducted and completed, the fee is non-refundable.

4. Technical Disruptions

In the event of unforeseen internet disruption or technical issues preventing a smooth online video session, the consultation will be resumed or rescheduled at the earliest mutually agreed time without penalty.

5. How to Request a Reschedule or Refund

To request a reschedule or cancellation, please notify us immediately with your booking name and phone number via WhatsApp or through our contact form.
