Living in Resonance with the Five Natural Elements.

Vastu Shastra is the venerable Indian architectural science that studies how spatial orientation, geomagnetic grids, and cosmic energy fields influence human consciousness, health, and prosperity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Vastu Consultations.

Clear, upfront answers about floor plans, non-demolition remedies, and site visits.

What exact details or documents are required for a Vastu consultation? +

To conduct a precise Vastu analysis, the following details are required:

  • Scaled Floor Plan: An architectural blueprint, builder brochure plan, or accurately measured sketch showing room dimensions, wall boundaries, and door/window placements.
  • Exact Magnetic North: A compass reading taken from the center of the property or main entrance.
  • Functional Zones: Clear marking of current kitchen cooktop, sink, toilets, bed locations, and water tanks (overhead and underground).
  • Photographs or Video: Brief video clips or photos of the rooms and main entryway for remote consultations.
Does Vastu consultation require knocking down walls or breaking existing structures? +

No, absolutely not. Priyanka Dey specializes in ethical, non-demolition Vastu solutions (Vastu Dosh Nivaran). For fully built residential properties, apartments, and commercial offices, structural demolition is almost never practical, safe, or cost-effective.

Instead, spatial imbalances are harmonized through non-invasive techniques: elemental balancing (adjusting colors, lighting, and natural materials), brass/copper/zinc metallic boundary partitions, directional mirrors, sacred geometric yantras, and functional furniture realignments.

Can Vastu principles be applied effectively to modern high-rise apartments and rented flats? +

Yes. While modern high-rise flats share common building walls and fixed plumbing conduits, you hold complete sovereignty over your internal micro-environment.

Through internal zoning, we optimize where you sleep (head direction), where children study, which direction your work desk faces, and how elemental energies are balanced within individual rooms. These subtle adjustments produce noticeable enhancements in peace of mind, sleep quality, and domestic vitality without requiring landlord permissions.

What is the Brahmasthan, and why is it so important in a home or office? +

The Brahmasthan is the exact geometric center and vibrational heart of any architectural boundary, governed by the Akash (Space/Ether) element. In traditional Indian architecture, it was designed as an open-to-sky central courtyard.

In modern construction, the Brahmasthan must be kept as light, clean, and uncluttered as possible. Locating heavy structural pillars, staircases, toilets, or waste storage in the Brahmasthan blocks the natural circulation of Pranic energy throughout the dwelling, often manifesting as widespread chronic fatigue or unexplained friction among family members.

Which direction is considered most auspicious for the main entrance of a property? +

While general popular belief holds that only North or East entrances are auspicious, classical Vastu Shastra calculates 32 distinct entrance padas (sectors) encircling the perimeter.

Each cardinal direction possesses highly favorable entrance portals: for example, specific sectors in the West (governed by Varuna and Mitra) bring sustained commercial success, while specific sectors in the South (such as Grihakshata) bestow immense stability and reputation. What matters is the exact mathematical degree of the entrance, rather than a broad general direction.

How does commercial Vastu improve business growth, employee productivity, and cash flow? +

In a commercial enterprise, Vastu aligns corporate function with environmental energy flows:

  • Executive Cabin (South-West): Anchors the leadership team with decisive authority, vision, and stability.
  • Finance & Accounts (North): The realm of Kubera, facilitating steady receivables, positive cash flow, and financial liquidity.
  • Sales & Dispatch (North-West): The domain of Vayu (Air), driving fast client turnaround, active networking, and inventory movement.
  • Reception & Innovation (North-East): Keeps client first impressions inspiring and executive strategy clear and forward-looking.
