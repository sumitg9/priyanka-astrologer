Hello, I’m Priyanka Dey.

I offer personalised consultations across Vedic astrology, tarot reading, Vastu Shastra and numerology for individuals seeking clarity, alignment, and calm perspective on the questions that matter to them.

My aim is to cultivate a respectful, reflective space where you can share your concerns, understand traditional astrological and spatial principles, and make informed choices with peace of mind.

Based in Siliguri, West Bengal, I welcome clients for in-person appointments by arrangement, as well as online video consultations across India and worldwide.
