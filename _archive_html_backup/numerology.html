Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Numerology.

Clear, upfront answers about birth numbers, name vibrations, and annual cycles.

What exact details are required for a personal numerology consultation? +

To conduct a thorough numerology analysis, you only need to provide two key pieces of information:

  • Full Date of Birth: Day, month, and year of birth. (Unlike astrology, exact birth minute and location are not strictly necessary, making numerology accessible even if birth times are unknown).
  • Full Current Name: The exact spelling of your full legal name as well as your daily social name or signature spelling.
What is the fundamental difference between Mulank (Root Number) and Bhagyank (Destiny Number)? +

In Vedic Sankhya Shastra, these two numbers form your core numerical axis:

  • Mulank (Root / Driver Number): Calculated purely from your birth day (e.g. if born on the 28th: 2 + 8 = 10 -> 1). It governs your innate personality, daily habits, psychological instincts, and personal self-perception.
  • Bhagyank (Destiny / Life Path Number): Calculated from the entire sum of day + month + year. It represents your worldly destiny, career milestones, opportunities that life brings, and your mature calling after age 30.
Can changing or adjusting the spelling of my name really make a difference? +

Yes, when performed thoughtfully and grounded in classical acoustic principles. Every letter in your name corresponds to a specific numerical vibration and planetary resonance.

If your Name Number (Namank) clashes with your natal Mulank or Bhagyank, it can create chronic misunderstandings, delayed recognition, or friction in business endeavors. By introducing subtle, elegant spelling adjustments, your name frequency harmonizes with your birth numbers, smoothing social interactions and removing energetic friction.

Which numerology system do you follow: Chaldean, Pythagorean, or Vedic? +

Priyanka Dey synthesizes both Vedic Sankhya Shastra and the venerable Chaldean system.

The Vedic system provides profound planetary archetype insights for digits 1 through 9, while the Chaldean system calculates letter frequencies based on acoustic vibration (sound energy) rather than arbitrary alphabetical order. This dual approach yields significantly more accurate, nuanced results than Western Pythagorean systems alone.

What is a Personal Year cycle, and how does it help in annual planning? +

In numerological forecasting, life operates in 9-year epicycles. Your Personal Year (Varshik Ank) indicates the specific seasonal lesson and focus of your current calendar year:

  • Year 1: Initiating new ventures, career pivots, and bold independence.
  • Year 4: Hard structural groundwork, discipline, and establishing stability.
  • Year 5: Dynamic expansion, travel, sales growth, and sudden shifts.
  • Year 8: Financial harvest, corporate authority, and karmic consolidation.
  • Year 9: Completion, releasing outmoded relationships, and spiritual preparation for the next 9-year cycle.
Can numerology help in selecting auspicious business names, brand names, or baby names? +

Yes, this is one of the most rewarding practical applications of numerology. For commercial enterprises, we calculate the compound numerical value of prospective company names, brand identities, and domain names to ensure strong resonance with the founder's charts and customer attraction.

For newborn children, choosing a name with a harmonious Namank aligns with the baby's Mulank and birth Nakshatra, blessing the child with lifelong vibrational harmony.
