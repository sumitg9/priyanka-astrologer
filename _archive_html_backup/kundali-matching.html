Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Kundali Matching.

Clear, upfront answers about Guna scores, Mangal Dosha, Nadi cancellations, and marriage consultations.

What exact details are required for Kundali Matching? +

To generate an authentic, mathematically sound compatibility report, exact birth coordinates are required for both individuals:

  • Date of Birth: Day, month, and year for both partners.
  • Exact Time of Birth: Hours and minutes (as recorded in hospital birth records) to establish correct rising Lagna and Navamsha degrees.
  • Place of Birth: Town, city, or district for both individuals to calculate accurate geographic latitude and longitude.
How many Gunas out of 36 are considered acceptable for a harmonious marriage? +

In the classical Ashtakoot Milan system, a minimum score of 18 out of 36 Gunas is considered the baseline for acceptable compatibility.

  • 18 to 24 Gunas: Moderate to good compatibility with manageable differences.
  • 25 to 32 Gunas: Very strong compatibility indicating deep mutual resonance and shared life values.
  • 33 to 36 Gunas: Exceptionally rare and highly auspicious union.

However, Guna score is only one element of a full reading. Individual 7th houses, longevity, and planetary strengths must always be evaluated in conjunction.

What if our Guna score is below 18? Can we still marry? +

Yes. Guna Milan calculates only Moon-to-Moon compatibility. Classical Vedic Jyotish recognizes numerous cancellation rules (Parihara).

If the individual 7th house lords, Venus, Jupiter, and Navamsha (D9) charts show strong mutual support, emotional maturity, and planetary friendship, a couple can enjoy a flourishing, enduring marriage even with a lower Guna count. Priyanka explains these nuances clearly so couples can proceed with confidence.

How is Manglik Dosha evaluated during marriage matching? +

Manglik Dosha occurs when Mars occupies houses 1, 4, 7, 8, or 12 in the natal chart. While widely feared due to commercial myths, classical scriptures describe dozens of cancellation factors (Mangal Dosha Bhanga):

  • Mars placed in its own sign (Aries or Scorpio) or exalted in Capricorn cancels the dosha.
  • Mars aspected by a strong benefic Jupiter cancels major negative manifestations.
  • If both partners have comparable Mars placements, the fiery energies balance each other out naturally.
What is Nadi Dosha, and can it be remedied or cancelled? +

Nadi carries 8 points in Ashtakoot Milan and relates to genetic temperament, physiological health, and progeny. If both partners share the same Nadi (Adya, Madhya, or Antya), Nadi Dosha is indicated.

However, classical scriptures provide multiple cancellation rules: sharing the same Rashi with different Nakshatras, or having different Rashis under the same Nakshatra, completely neutralizes Nadi Dosha. Priyanka thoroughly verifies these exceptions before recommending any remedies.

Why is automated online Guna Milan not sufficient on its own? +

Free online matching tools only perform simple arithmetic on Moon signs, completely ignoring:

  • The individual 7th house and its lord in both birth charts.
  • The crucial D9 Navamsha chart (the ultimate chart for marital bliss and longevity).
  • Active Vimshottari Mahadashas and future planetary periods.
  • The Upapada Lagna and mutual planetary aspects.

A personalized consultation with an experienced Vedic astrologer replaces automated fear with empathetic, comprehensive guidance.

Both families or the prospective couple can join the session together to review both charts with live screen sharing, followed by a written summary of all findings for your records.
