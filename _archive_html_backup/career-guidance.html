Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Career Guidance.

Clear, upfront answers about vocational analysis, D10 Dashamsha, job changes, and business timing.

How can Vedic astrology determine the most suitable career field for me? +

Vedic astrology evaluates vocational suitability through a multi-factor matrix:

  • The 10th House (Karma Bhava): The zodiac sign on the 10th cusp, planets placed in it, and its ruling lord's placement.
  • The Amatyakaraka: The planet with the second-highest astronomical degree in your chart, which acts as your personal vocational significator.
  • The D10 Dashamsha chart: The harmonic division of profession revealing where your efforts receive worldly recognition.

For example, strong Mars-Sun connections indicate governance, engineering, or executive administration, while Jupiter-Mercury alignments indicate finance, education, law, or strategic advisory.

Should I pursue a salaried job or start my own business? +

This fundamental question is resolved by comparing your 6th House (service, subordination, routine employment, technical expertise) with your 7th House (public trading, business partnerships) and 10th/11th Houses (independent executive authority and commercial profits).

If your chart possesses strong entrepreneurial yogas with benefic planetary support in houses 7, 10, and 11, independent enterprise or consulting will bring far greater fulfillment and wealth. Conversely, if the 6th house is dominant with strong Sun/Saturn placements, corporate leadership or civil service often provides the ideal trajectory.

Can astrology predict the right time for a job change, salary hike, or promotion? +

Yes. Career elevations occur when favorable Vimshottari Dasha periods align with supportive planetary transits (such as Jupiter aspecting your 10th or 11th house).

Priyanka identifies the specific months when approaching your superiors, negotiating salary increases, or interviewing with prospective employers carries maximum momentum, helping you avoid switching during unsupportive planetary seasons.

What is the Dashamsha (D10) chart, and why is it essential for career guidance? +

The Dashamsha (D10) is the tenth harmonic divisional chart in classical Vedic astrology, dedicated exclusively to career, leadership, professional achievements, and public status.

While the standard D1 birth chart shows overall life potential, the D10 chart zooms directly into your workplace environment, showing how you handle workplace authority, whether you receive institutional backing, and how your career evolves in its mature stages.

How can astrology help before entering a business partnership or signing major contracts? +

Commercial partnerships carry substantial financial and legal stakes. By cross-examining co-founders' or business partners' horoscopes, Priyanka evaluates:

  • Mutual trust indicators and complementary skill sets.
  • Shared financial fortune and collective Dhana Yogas.
  • Potential friction periods to ensure contractual agreements are structured with foresight.
I am experiencing prolonged career stagnation or sudden job loss. How can a reading help? +

Prolonged professional plateaus or unexpected corporate restructuring often coincide with transit pressure from Saturn (Shani Gochara) or Rahu/Ketu shifts across your career axis.

A consultation provides emotional peace by explaining the exact timeline of the cycle, offering practical strategies to acquire new skills during the lull, and providing classical Vedic remedies to soften planetary friction.
