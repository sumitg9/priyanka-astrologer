Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Our Services.

Clear, upfront answers about session formats, preparation, remedies, and choosing the right discipline.

How do I choose between an Astrology, Tarot, Vastu, or Numerology consultation? +

Each discipline addresses a distinct facet of your life:

  • Astrology / Janam Kundali: Ideal when you have exact birth details and seek long-term clarity on career trajectories, Mahadasha cycles, relationship compatibility, and major life timing.
  • Tarot Reading: Best suited for immediate crossroads dilemmas, emotional reflections, and situations where exact birth time is unavailable.
  • Vastu Shastra: Recommended for harmonizing physical living environments, improving domestic tranquility, or optimizing corporate workplaces for financial liquidity.
  • Numerology: Excellent for optimizing name spellings, brand names, understanding your core Root Number (Mulank), and navigating your 9-year personal cycle.
Can multiple disciplines be combined into a single consultation session? +

Yes. Priyanka frequently synthesizes disciplines during comprehensive consultations. For instance, an Astrology session can be enriched with Numerological name frequency analysis, or Astrological transit timing can be paired with an intuitive Tarot spread to explore personal crossroads in depth.

What is the difference between an in-person chamber consultation and an online video reading? +

The astrological calculation depth, consultation duration (60 minutes), and personal attention are 100% identical. In-person consultations take place at our quiet chamber in Siliguri, West Bengal.

Online sessions are conducted live via Google Meet or WhatsApp Video Call with screen-sharing capabilities, allowing clients across India and internationally to view their charts in real time.

What if I do not have my exact birth time? Which services can I still book? +

If your exact birth time is completely unknown, you can book:

  • Tarot Reading: Requires no birth data; works through real-time energetic resonance.
  • Numerology: Requires only the date of birth (day, month, year) and full legal name.
  • Vastu Shastra: Evaluates spatial floor plans and compass directions, independent of personal birth charts.
  • Prashna Kundali (Horary Astrology): Cast for the exact moment you ask your question.

If your birth time is known within an approximate window, Birth Time Rectification (BTR) can also be conducted.

How should I prepare my questions before attending a consultation? +

We recommend writing down 3 to 5 clear, prioritized questions before your session. Open-ended inquiries—such as “What factors should I weigh before switching careers?” or “How can we resolve persistent communication friction in our marriage?”—yield far deeper, more actionable guidance than simple yes/no questions.

How strictly confidential are consultation discussions? +

Confidentiality is an uncompromising ethical pillar of our practice. Your birth coordinates, personal life circumstances, family concerns, and session notes are kept completely confidential and are never disclosed, recorded publicly, or shared with any third party.

What is your policy regarding remedies—are there hidden rituals or mandatory purchases? +

No. Priyanka Dey adheres strictly to ethical Vedic and philosophical standards and firmly opposes fear-mongering commercialism.

Remedies suggested are classical, sattvic, and empowering: personalized Vedic mantras for mental tranquility, charitable donations (Daana), behavioral adjustments, and gemstone guidance only when genuinely beneficial and feasible for the client.
