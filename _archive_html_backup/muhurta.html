Electional Methodology

The Five Sacred Pillars of Vedic Muhurta (Panchanga).

How our electional methodology aligns the 5 elemental limbs of time for maximum protection and fruitfulness.

☽ 01 / Water Element (Jala)

Tithi (Lunar Phase)

Tithi dictates emotional harmony and financial fruition. We eliminate Rikta tithis (4th, 9th, 14th) and choose Nanda, Bhadra, and Jaya phases aligned with growth and stability.

☀ 02 / Fire Element (Agni)

Vara (Solar Day)

Each weekday channels the raw kinetic energy of its planetary ruler. Sun and Mars empower authority and physical starts, while Mercury, Jupiter, and Venus support commercial and cultural milestones.

SELECT AUSPICIOUS DAY ↗
✦ 03 / Air Element (Vayu)

Nakshatra (Lunar Mansion)

The Moon's stellar constellation defines the subtle psychological vibe. Fixed (Dhruva) nakshatras stabilize properties, while Movable (Chara) constellations facilitate trade and rapid growth.

HARMONIZE NAKSHATRA ↗
⚛ 04 / Ether Element (Akasha)

Yoga (Luni-Solar Harmony)

Yoga reflects the blended synergy of Sun and Moon angles. We screen out malevolent yogas like Vyatipata and Vaidhriti, ensuring auspicious yogas like Siddha and Amrita prevail.

FILTER LUNI-SOLAR YOGA ↗
⚒ 05 / Earth Element (Prithvi)

Karana (Half-Tithi Execution)

Governed by Earth, Karana directs material outcome. We rigorously avoid Vishti (Bhadra Karana) to prevent administrative stalemates, disputes, or sudden operational bottlenecks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Muhurta Selection.

Clear, straightforward answers about electing auspicious times and conducting successful ceremonies.

What is Muhurta and why is selecting an auspicious time important? +

Muhurta is the classical Vedic science of electional astrology (Muhurta Shastra). Just as a seed planted in fertile soil during the right season yields an abundant harvest, an action initiated at a celestial moment charged with planetary harmony naturally avoids obstacles, minimizes friction, and maximizes prosperity and success.

Can a favorable Muhurta overcome difficult planetary placements in my birth chart? +

While your natal Kundali outlines your core karmic blueprint, a thoughtfully chosen Muhurta functions as an astrological shield. By choosing a rising ascendant (Lagna) that strengthens beneficial planets and pacifies negative natal transits, a strong Muhurta significantly mitigates adversity and safeguards your venture.

What if the suggested Muhurta falls at an inconvenient hour or date? +

Priyanka Dey provides practical, tiered Muhurta options. You will receive primary ideal windows alongside secondary viable fallback options within your preferred calendar range, ensuring you can coordinate venues, guests, and logistical arrangements realistically without compromising spiritual alignment.

What is the difference between a general calendar Panchang and a personalized Muhurta? +

Standard astrological calendars and almanacs provide generic timings based on geographic averages. However, a generic 'good day' can be personally destructive if the Moon transits your 8th house (Ashtama Chandra) or activates an adverse Tara Bala. Our personalized Muhurta cross-references the celestial sky directly against the exact birth charts of the key participants.

What details are required to calculate a personalized Muhurta? +

We require the birth details (date, exact time, and city of birth) of the principal participants (e.g. bride & groom for marriage; founders for business; property owners for Griha Pravesh), the target event location, and your preferred date window range.

How far in advance should I request a Muhurta selection? +

For major events like weddings (Vivaha) or large commercial launches, it is recommended to request a Muhurta 2 to 6 months in advance to secure preferred venues and vendors. For property signings or vehicle deliveries, a window of 1 to 2 weeks is usually sufficient.

Does Muhurta selection avoid Rahu Kaal and Yamaganda? +

Yes. Every custom Muhurta rigorously eliminates inauspicious daily periods including Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Durmuhurta, and Bhadra (Vishti Karana), ensuring that only pristine, constructive energy supports your beginning.
