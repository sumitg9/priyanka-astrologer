Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Relationship Guidance.

Clear, straightforward answers about our Love & Marriage Astrology consultation process and principles.

Can Vedic astrology predict whether I will have a love marriage or an arranged marriage? +

Yes, Vedic astrology examines the interplay between the 5th House (House of romance, emotional attachment, and intellect) and the 7th House (House of marriage and committed partnership). When the lords of the 5th and 7th houses exchange signs, aspect each other, or form beneficial yogas with Venus and Mars, love marriage is strongly indicated. Favourable 9th house connections also show familial blessing and traditional solemnization.

What if my partner and I have low Guna Milan points? Can this consultation help? +

Yes. Guna Milan is only a preliminary 36-point lunar filter and represents just 15% of true Vedic relationship compatibility. A full consultation dives far deeper into the Navamsha (D9) chart, 7th house longevity, emotional temperament, mutual planetary friendship, and planetary periods (Dasha). Many couples with lower Guna scores thrive beautifully once mutual astrological dynamics and remedial steps are understood.

How do you analyze delays in marriage (Vivaha Vilambha)? +

Marital delays are analyzed by studying the placement and aspects of Saturn, Rahu, Mars, or the Sun on the 7th House or its lord, along with afflictions in the Navamsha chart. We also check the current Mahadasha-Antardasha cycles. We pinpoint the exact astrological window when auspicious dasha periods activate the marital houses, while prescribing Vedic remedial measures to clear karmic delays.

Does being Manglik (Mangal Dosha) mean my marriage is doomed? +

Absolutely not. Mangal Dosha is widely misunderstood and sensationalized. Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house simply denotes intense passion, high energetic drive, and assertiveness. In Vedic astrology, over 75% of Manglik placements possess natural scriptural cancellations (Apavada) based on sign placement, planetary aspects, or partner chart balance.

Can I consult if my partner is unwilling to share their birth details? +

Yes. Your personal birth chart contains deep, revealing information about your partnership karma, spouse characteristics, emotional triggers, and timing cycles through your 7th House, Upapada Lagna, and Navamsha chart. We can evaluate your personal relationship karma and provide empowering guidance even without your partner's chart.

What is the difference between Janam Kundali (D1) and Navamsha (D9) for marriage? +

The Janam Kundali (D1) represents the outer physical reality, general life potential, and the circumstances under which relationships begin. The Navamsha (D9), however, is the soul-level chart. It reveals how the relationship will mature after marriage, your emotional contentment, spiritual bond, and the inner nature of your spouse.

What kind of remedies do you recommend for relationship healing? +

All remedies suggested by Priyanka Dey are peaceful, non-dogmatic, and satvic. These include planetary mantras for Venus and Jupiter, specific days of fasting or charity, spatial harmony adjustments in the master bedroom based on Vastu, and conscious behavioral adjustments to neutralize negative karmic friction.
