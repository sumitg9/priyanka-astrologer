The Archetypal Mirror

Tarot as an Intuitive Gateway to Subconscious Clarity.

A tarot consultation is not passive fortune-telling; it is a sacred, symbolic dialogue. Through the timeless visual language of 78 archetypal cards, a reading acts as a psychological and intuitive mirror—reflecting your current emotional landscape, unspoken tensions, and latent possibilities.

Where logic often hits a wall in moments of transition or confusion, the cards bypass conscious overthinking. Priyanka Dey creates a tranquil, non-judgmental space to examine what is really occurring beneath surface anxieties, empowering you to reconnect with your own inner agency.

  • 78 Archetypal Keys: 22 Major Arcana soul lessons combined with 56 Minor Arcana situational cards.
  • Crossroads Clarity: Unpack choices between distinct career paths, partnerships, or relocation decisions.
  • No Birth Time Needed: An ideal intuitive consultation format when exact birth coordinates are unavailable.
  • Astro-Tarot Synergy: Enriching card interpretations with planetary rulerships and elemental wisdom.
Empowerment Philosophy: Tarot cards never dictate an unavoidable fate. They map prevailing energies and likely outcomes based on your present trajectory. You always possess sovereign free will to alter your course.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Tarot Readings.

Clear, upfront answers to common questions about preparing for your tarot session.

How does a tarot card reading work, and what can it tell me? +

Tarot operates as an intuitive mirror to the subconscious mind through 78 archetypal cards (22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana). When you pose a heartfelt question, the layout of cards captures the energetic and psychological patterns surrounding your situation.

Rather than fortune-telling in a fatalistic sense, Tarot illuminates what is currently hidden from your conscious view: emotional blind spots, underlying partner dynamics, prospective opportunities, and the most constructive path forward.

How does Tarot differ from Vedic Astrology? +

The primary differences lie in astronomical mechanics and temporal focus:

  • Vedic Astrology (Jyotish): Requires your exact birth date, time, and location to calculate planetary dignities, 12 Bhavas, and lifelong Vimshottari Mahadashas. It maps long-term karmic seasons and timing.
  • Tarot Reading: Requires no birth time coordinates whatsoever. It captures the immediate, real-time vibrational snapshot of your present crossroads, emotions, and practical decision forks.

Tarot is particularly invaluable when exact birth times are unknown, or when you need immediate clarity on a specific dilemma right now.

Can Tarot predict my exact future or fix my destiny? +

No, and an ethical practitioner will never claim to do so. The cards reveal where current energies, intentions, and subconscious habits are leading. They indicate the most probable outcome if nothing changes.

However, you are endowed with conscious free will. If a card spread highlights an impending obstacle or disharmonious trajectory, the purpose of the reading is precisely to empower you to adjust your choices, communicate differently, and create a more fulfilling outcome.

What kinds of questions are best suited for a tarot consultation? +

Open-ended, reflective questions produce the most nuanced and transformative readings:

  • Career & Purpose: “What factors should I weigh before choosing between Opportunity A and Opportunity B?” or “How can I overcome my current professional stagnation?”
  • Love & Relationships: “What dynamics are currently influencing our connection?” or “What personal healing is required before entering a new partnership?”
  • Personal Growth: “What blind spot is currently holding me back?” or “How can I cultivate deeper inner resilience?”
Are Tarot readings scary, negative, or superstitious? +

Not in the slightest. Popular media often dramatizes cards like Death, The Tower, or The Devil to evoke cheap suspense. In authentic esoteric tarot, these cards carry deeply liberating, psychological meanings:

  • Death (Card XIII): Represents natural metamorphosis, closing outmoded cycles, and clearing space for vibrant new beginnings.
  • The Tower (Card XVI): Represents breakthrough, shattering restrictive illusions, and sudden liberating awakening.
  • The Devil (Card XV): Illuminates unhealthy attachments, obsessive habits, or self-imposed mental limits so they can be dissolved.

Priyanka conducts all sessions with empathy, maturity, and compassionate common sense.

Can I do a Tarot reading online via video call? Does it work as effectively? +

Yes, absolutely. Intuitive attunement and archetypal symbolism are not bound by physical distance. Online video sessions via Google Meet or WhatsApp Video are just as intimate, interactive, and accurate as sitting in our chamber.

During the session, the camera clearly focuses on the physical card deck as cards are shuffled, cut, and laid out in front of you. You participate actively in formulating queries and exploring the spread.

How private and confidential are tarot readings? +

Absolute confidentiality is guaranteed. All questions, personal life circumstances, relationship vulnerabilities, and card discussion details remain strictly private between you and Priyanka Dey. We never record, publish, or discuss client consultations under any circumstances.
